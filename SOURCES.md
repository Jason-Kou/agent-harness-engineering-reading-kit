- The primary study compares pinned architectures; it is not a shared-model, shared-task benchmark.
- The “0/11” observations apply only to the selected systems and versions.
- Vendor engineering articles explain design experience; they are not independent performance evidence.
- Third-party works are linked, not relicensed or redistributed here.
